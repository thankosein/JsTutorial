**Date and Time Inputs** in HTML are useful when you want the user to pick a date, time, or both without relying on free-text entry — which can lead to invalid or inconsistent formats. These input types are **built into HTML5** and work with the browser’s native date/time pickers (on desktop, and especially on mobile where it shows a nice calendar or time selector). --- ## **When to Use** You’d typically use date/time inputs when: 1. **Booking or Scheduling** * Hotel reservations, flight bookings, meeting schedulers. * Example: `type="date"` for check-in and check-out dates. 2. **Deadlines & Expiration Dates** * Event registration deadlines, product expiry dates. 3. **Data Filtering** * Reports filtered by start/end date ranges. 4. **Logging & Tracking** * Record when something happened (timestamp). --- ## **Types of Date/Time Inputs** | Type | Example | Description | | ---------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | | `date` | 📅 2025-08-15 | Select a date (no time). | | `time` | ⏰ 14:30 | Select a time (no date). | | `datetime-local` | 📅⏰ 2025-08-15T14:30 | Select both date and time (local timezone). | | `month` | 📆 2025-08 | Select month & year only. | | `week` | 📅 Week 33, 2025 | Select a specific week of the year. | --- ## **Example** ```html
